# Communication playbook: from viewer to regular

Posts create attention. This stage turns attention into bookings and keeps students coming back. Written in Saj & Emi's voice: short, warm, one question at a time. Replace anything in [brackets]. Never quote a price that the owner has not confirmed.

## The funnel and who handles each step

| Stage | Channel | Run by | Owner effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Comment | Instagram comments | Claude drafts weekly via Supermetrics; Reply Bank mode posts approved templates automatically | Approve the Reply Bank once |
| 2. DM keyword | Instagram DMs | Instagram's own automations (keyword replies), set up once with the scripts below | 20 min one-time setup |
| 3. Booking | WhatsApp | The owner or a team member, using saved quick replies | Seconds per lead |
| 4. First class | WhatsApp | Quick replies: reminder, after-class follow-up | Seconds per student |
| 5. Package and level | WhatsApp + class | Quick replies at week 1, week 4 and graduation | Seconds per student |
| 6. Retention | WhatsApp | Missed-class nudge after 2 missed classes | Seconds per student |
| 7. Advocacy | WhatsApp + Instagram | Review, bring-a-friend and festival invites | Seconds per student |

Instagram DMs cannot be read or sent from this workspace (the Supermetrics connection covers posts and comments only). DM automation therefore lives in Instagram's own settings: Meta Business Suite → Inbox → Automations, or the Instagram app → Settings → Business tools → Automated responses. For "Comment KEYWORD and we'll DM you" posts, the paid tool ManyChat can send the DM automatically; that is the owner's call.

## 1. Comment Reply Bank (owner approves once, then Claude posts these automatically)

Only for simple, positive comments. Anything with a question about price, a complaint, a personal story or anything unclear goes to the owner.

| Comment type | Reply |
|---|---|
| Emoji or praise | "Thank you! 🙌 Who would you dance this with?" |
| "I need this" / "saving this" | "Save it and try it at your next social. Tell us how it goes 💃" |
| Tags a friend | "Bring them on Sunday 😉 First class is the hardest step." |
| "I can't dance" / "too shy" | "That's exactly who our Sunday Foundation class is for. No partner needed." |
| Shares their own nerves story | Goes to the owner (needs a personal reply). |
| Asks time or location | "Sundays 8 PM at Dance Case Studio, JLT. DM us START and we'll send the details." |

## 2. DM keyword scripts (paste into Instagram automations)

**START / CLASS / SUNDAY**
> Hey [name]! So happy you want to start 💃 Our Foundation class is on Sundays at 8 PM at Dance Case Studio, JLT (Fortune Executive Tower, Cluster T). No partner or experience needed. Want me to save you a spot this Sunday? Just reply YES and send your WhatsApp number.

**SOCIAL**
> Hey [name]! Going out to dance? 🔥 Our Latin nights: Thursday Room 44, Friday SoBe, Saturday TT DXB. If you're new to socials, come to Sunday Foundation first and we'll get you ready. Want the details for this week?

**PRACTICE**
> Monday Practice Session is at D World Dance Studio, AED 30, [time]. All levels dance together, it's the best way to lock in what you learn. Shall I add you to this Monday's list?

**BACHATA / DANCE** (general)
> Hey [name]! Are you completely new to bachata, or have you danced before? That way I can tell you which class fits you best.

**BACHAZOUK**
> BachaZouk is bachata with zouk's flow 🌊 Our workshops and socials are on @bachazouk.dubai. Have you danced bachata before? Then I'll tell you which session to start with.

**FESTIVAL**
> The Latin Crazy Festival is 1–5 April 2027 in Dubai 🎉 Want me to send you the pass details and pre-party dates as soon as they're out?

## 3. WhatsApp sequence (save each as a quick reply)

## Labels to use in WhatsApp Business
New lead · Trial booked · Attended trial · Package · Missed 2 · Graduated · Festival interest

## What Claude does every week
- Monday run: pull comments on the last week's posts, post Reply Bank replies (once enabled), and list anything that needs the owner.
- Monthly report: ask the owner for counts by label (new leads, trials, packages, missed-2, graduates) and track the three retention numbers: trial → package, Foundation → Improvers, and students active after 90 days.
