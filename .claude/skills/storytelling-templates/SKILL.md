# Storytelling Templates (Dubai Bachata + Latin Crazy Tribe)

10 first-person story formats for short-form video. Full beat sheets and fill-in lines: `references/formats.md`. **Pick the account first**: `references/account-playbooks.md` sets the goal, tone, format priority, hooks, CTAs and guardrails for @dubaibachata (informative) and @latincrazytribe (party). The selector below is the @dubaibachata view; the party ranking is in the playbook. Source: "Storytelling Template Database", Short Form Academy (owner's copy, uploaded 11 Nov 2026), adapted.

## Pick the format in 30 seconds

Start from **what raw material you actually have**, not from the format you like.

| You have… | Format | Best for (pillar → KPI) |
|---|---|---|
| A student who struggled, then got it | 1. Hero's Journey | Student progress → bookings |
| A result + the non-obvious method behind it | 2. Personal Learning / Epiphany | Move fixes, mindset → saves |
| Saj's or Emi's "why I dance / why I teach" | 3. About Me (Origin) | Pinned post → trust, follows |
| Two contrasting states (week 1 vs week 8) | 4. Before & After | Student progress → shares, bookings |
| A big open-ended aim (festival stage, showcase) | 5. Goal / Dream Journey | Festival road → follows, series |
| A bounded mission with rules and a deadline | 6. Challenge | Entertainment → watch time, series |
| A milestone with visible proof | 7. Win | Event proof, graduations → tickets |
| A full day (class day, festival prep) | 8. Day in the Life | Saj & Emi on camera → follows |
| News: new group, new venue, festival reveal | 9. Personal Update | Community → comments |
| A lesson from a teacher, a Dominican musician, a student | 10. Mentor Story | Heritage, mindset → saves, trust |

Tie-breakers: faceless track first (owner rule, 3 Nov). Formats 2, 4, 6 and 10 work fully faceless with the mascot. Formats 3, 8 and 9 need Saj & Emi on camera or their real voice, so plan them for the Thursday shoot.

## Universal spine (every format)

1. **Hook** (0–3 s): from `viral-hook-writer` + `negative-hooks`, passed through the `virality` 8-point check.
2. **Character + stakes**: who, and what they stand to lose or gain. The viewer must see themselves (nervous beginner, no partner, "too old", "two left feet").
3. **Tension**: failed attempts, obstacle, complication. No tension = no retention.
4. **Turn**: the solution, epiphany, reveal or result.
5. **Payoff**: proof the turn worked (visual, not claimed).
6. **CTA** (optional, last 3 s): one DM keyword or "Follow for a new move every week". Class details only here, never in the hook.

## Outlier rules (the "secret sauce", per format)

| Format | What makes it an outlier |
|---|---|
| Hero's Journey | Viewer feels the pain first. Exaggerate the failed attempts; the fix lands only if the struggle was felt. |
| Personal Learning | Undeniable visual proof up front, then a non-obvious method. Obvious method = "they're just talented". |
| Origin | Personal, not professional. Pin it. Builds trust, not authority. |
| Before & After | Size of the gap. Cut on the beat drop. 15–30 s, no explaining. |
| Goal / Dream | Ambitious enough that people doubt you. Show vulnerable moments. Invite them to follow along. |
| Challenge | Grand objective + real complications. End on result or cliffhanger (part 2). |
| Win | Raw, spontaneous, proof on screen. Short. |
| Day in the Life | Something unexpected about the life. They should want your life or like your personality. |
| Personal Update | Behind the curtain. Community, not authority. |
| Mentor Story | Make the other person vivid and named; land the lesson as a direct quote. You're the student, not the hero. |

## Dubai Bachata guardrails (check every story)

- **Truth only.** Never invent a student, quote, result, number or testimonial. Story slots that need a real fact are marked `[OWNER: …]` and stay open until the owner fills them.
- **Consent.** Real students are named or shown only with filming consent (collected at signup) and their OK on the specific story.
- **Heritage check** (from `dubai-bachata-social`): Mentor and Origin stories often touch history or culture. Bachata is Dominican; credit it in the video and the caption; never "styles of bachata".
- **Tone.** Warm, cheeky, never shaming, never sensual. The struggling beginner is the hero we respect, not the joke.
- **Account separation.** No Bacha Fusion / BachaZouk / weekender material on @dubaibachata.
- **Humanizer pass** on every spoken and on-screen line before handoff.
