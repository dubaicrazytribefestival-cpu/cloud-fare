# Account playbooks: who the story is for

The story planner serves **2 primary accounts**. Every story brief names exactly one of them. The other 3 brand accounts (@latincrazyfestival.dubai, @bachazouk.dubai, and @sajyemi pending merge) get stories **only when the owner asks** in that request.

| | **@dubaibachata** | **@latincrazytribe** |
|---|---|---|
| Role | Informative: beginner classes, Saj & Emi, B-YOU Method, JLT | Party: Latin nights Thu Room 44 · Fri SoBe · Sat TT DXB, themed nights |
| Story job | Teach, build trust, turn a nervous beginner into a booking | Sell the feeling of the night, turn a scroller into a guest or table |
| Viewer's question | "Can someone like me actually do this?" | "Is this the place to be this week?" |
| Primary KPI | Saves, follows, DMs (START / CLASS / SUNDAY), trial bookings | Shares, tags, sends, "going" DMs, table enquiries |
| Hero of the story | The nervous beginner (viewer's mirror) or the teachers | The crowd and the night: first-timers, regulars, the DJ, the energy |
| Tone | Warm, patient, a little cheeky. Teacher who remembers being a beginner | High energy, inclusive, fun. Never exclusive or VIP-snobby |
| Pace | 25–35 s, VO-led, clear payoff | 7–20 s, music-led, cuts on the beat, text over VO |
| Track | Faceless first (mascot explainers), plus Thursday class shoots | Recorded first: real night footage. Faceless only for announcements and line-ups |

## Hooks per account

- **@dubaibachata:** the soft families from `negative-hooks` (mistakes, reality check, curiosity gap). About the viewer's fear: "Your basic looks stiff, and here's why."
- **@latincrazytribe:** FOMO, social proof and identity. Never shaming. Visual first: frame 0 is the packed floor, the drop or a spin. "This was 11:47 PM on a Thursday." / "POV: you came alone." / "Tell me you're from Dubai without telling me…" The text hook carries it; VO is optional.

## Guardrails

**Both accounts**
- No sensual framing. No competitor names. No package prices (AED 50 trial only, on @dubaibachata). Real facts only; unknowns become `[OWNER: ]`.
- Humanizer pass on every line. Heritage check whenever a story touches music, history, culture or "what bachata is". At parties, credit Dominican bachata in the same way.
- Collab rule: one face (@sajknightsbachata / @emi_dancer_dubai) + one amplifier (@dance_dubai_videos, @latinnightsdubai, @socialdancejournal).

**@latincrazytribe only**
- **Consent on crowd footage.** Party guests haven't signed the student consent. Anyone identifiable as the hero of a story needs their OK. Crowd shots go wide or are blurred. Flag every brief that features a guest.
- **The dance and the people are the product.** Drinks, bottles and venue luxury are never the hook or the story. Venue partners are named as the venue (Room 44, SoBe at W Dubai The Palm, TT DXB), never as a sponsor deal unless the owner confirms one.
- **No door or entry prices or table minimums** unless the owner gives them for that post. Default: "Tables on WhatsApp."
- **Keep the brands separate.** No BachaZouk or Bacha Fusion event material. Festival content only as a Personal Update teaser that points to @latincrazyfestival.dubai.
- **Data gap.** Supermetrics publishing and insights are connected for @dubaibachata only. @latincrazytribe needs its own Instagram Insights connection before the tracker can report its results or schedule its posts.

## The cross-account bridge (the funnel no competitor has)

The path is first class → weekly LCT parties → festival stage. Tell it across both accounts:
- **@dubaibachata → party:** stories end at the first social ("Your first social, minute by minute" → "See you Thursday at Room 44"). Collab the post with @latincrazytribe.
- **@latincrazytribe → classes:** first-timer stories end with "Can't dance yet? Sunday Foundation at 8 PM. DM START to @dubaibachata."
- **One hero, two cuts:** the same student story, filmed once. Cut A (@dubaibachata, Hero's Journey: the learning) and Cut B (@latincrazytribe, Before & After: the first social night). The brief lists both cuts.
