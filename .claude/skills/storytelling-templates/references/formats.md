# The 10 story formats: beats and fill-in lines

Each format: **Starting point · Beats · Fill-in lines · Dubai Bachata angle · Faceless?**
Fill-in lines are starting points. Rewrite them in Saj & Emi's voice and run the humanizer. `[ ]` = slot; `[OWNER: ]` = a real fact only the owner can supply.

---

## 1. Hero's Journey (1st-person problem → solution)
**Starting point:** a core problem that leads to a transformation.
**Beats:** Hook → character + problem → failed attempts (exaggerate) → novel solution → why it worked + result → optional CTA.

| Beat | Fill-in lines |
|---|---|
| Intro | "[X] months ago I [problem]." · "I was a [role] struggling with [problem], and I had no idea how to fix it." |
| Inflection | "Because of this I was [pain/symptoms]." · "It got so bad that I [lowest moment]." |
| Rising action | "I tried [fail 1], [fail 2] and [fail 3]. Nothing worked." · "For [time] I did what everyone said: [common advice]. Still nothing." |
| Climax | "Then I found the one thing that worked: [solution]." · "Then I discovered [solution], and everything changed." |
| Falling action | "Once I started doing this, [result]." · "In [timeframe] I went from [before] to [after]." |
| Resolution (opt.) | "That's why I started [service], to help people like me get [dream result]." · "Now I help [audience] go from [pain] to [dream result]. [CTA]" |

**Dubai Bachata angle:** a real student who "watched YouTube tutorials for months, froze at their first social, then found Foundation". Or Saj's own first social. Hero = the viewer's mirror.
**Faceless?** Yes, with the main mascot narrating as Saj, or a student's VO over B-roll.

## 2. Personal Learning / Epiphany (proof first, then teach)
**Starting point:** a core learning to share. Lead with proof, then the method.
**Beats:** Hook (proof) → backstory → insight → step-by-step breakdown → optional "why this differs from common advice" → CTA.

| Beat | Fill-in lines |
|---|---|
| Hook (proof) | "I [result] in [timeframe]. Here's exactly how." · "This one change got me [result]." · "[proof on screen]. Everyone asks how." |
| Backstory | "Before this I was [old state] and I thought [old belief]." · "For [time] I was stuck at [plateau] because I was [wrong approach]." |
| Insight | "Then I realised [realisation]." · "The mistake I was making was [mistake]. The fix was [fix]." |
| Breakdown | "Here's what I did: first [1], then [2], then [3]." |
| CTA | "Want the full breakdown? Comment [KEYWORD]." |

**Dubai Bachata angle:** "I stopped counting 1-2-3-tap and started listening for the güira." Move fixes, musicality, nerves. Proof = side-by-side clip.
**Faceless?** Yes. Strong fit for the FX explainer style.

## 3. About Me (Origin story)
**Starting point:** the backstory behind the brand or mission. Make one, pin it.
**Beats:** Hook → normal life → trigger event → epiphany → change (action + today) → purpose.

| Beat | Fill-in lines |
|---|---|
| Intro | "Hi, I'm [name]. [X] years ago I was a normal [role] living a normal life." · "Before all this I was just a [relatable identity] doing [activity]." |
| Conflict | "All that changed when [event]." · "Then one day [trigger], and I couldn't ignore it." |
| Epiphany | "That's when I realised [epiphany]." |
| Change | "So I [bold action]. Fast forward to today: [current result] and [lifestyle change]." |
| Purpose | "Now everything I do is about helping [who] get [what] without [common pain]." |

**Dubai Bachata angle:** `[OWNER: how Saj found bachata]`, `[OWNER: how Saj & Emi met / started teaching]`, why "Dance. Connect. Belong." Credit the Dominican roots of what they learned.
**Faceless?** No. Needs Saj and/or Emi, real voice. Thursday shoot.

## 4. Before & After (transformation reveal)
**Starting point:** two contrasting states. The gap is the story.
**Beats:** Hook → before (relatable) → tension (beat build, countdown) → after (cut on beat drop) → optional context → optional CTA. 15–30 s.

| Beat | Fill-in lines |
|---|---|
| Hook | "[X] weeks of [effort] in [Y] seconds." · "[Subject] before vs after [action]." · "Watch this." |
| Before | "This is where I started." · "[X] weeks ago: [before]." · or text overlay only |
| Transition | No words. Cut on the drop, a clap or a snap. |
| After | "And this is now." · "[X] weeks later." · or silence |
| Context | "[X] weeks of Sunday classes. Worth it." |
| CTA | "New Foundation group starts the first Sunday of every month." |

**Dubai Bachata angle:** same student, week 1 vs B-YOU graduation. Same basic, stiff vs relaxed. Class floor empty vs full at the LCT party.
**Faceless?** Partly. Needs real footage of both states, or a mascot/animated version (FX-3 "same basic, no robot").

## 5. Goal / Dream Journey (open-ended, follow along)
**Starting point:** a long-term dream being pursued now. Not bounded like a Challenge.
**Beats:** Hook → the dream + why it matters → the moment you decided → progress so far → what's next / deadline → CTA "follow to see if…".

| Beat | Fill-in lines |
|---|---|
| Dream | "For as long as I can remember I've wanted to [dream]." |
| Pursuit | "After years of dreaming, on a random [day] I decided it was now or never. I was terrified, but I did it." |
| Progress | "So far I've [done]. Next I need to [step]. The deadline is [date]." |
| CTA | "Can we pull this off by [date]? Follow along." · "Comment YES if you think we can." |

**Dubai Bachata angle:** "Road to the LCT stage": Foundation students aiming for the festival student showcase on **26–29 March 2027**. A natural weekly series from now to March.
**Faceless?** Mixed. Students on camera are stronger; mascot-led progress cards work between shoots.

## 6. Challenge (bounded mission, rules, deadline)
**Starting point:** a mission with rules and a deadline. Vlog meets DIY.
**Beats:** Hook (challenge + stakes) → rules/setup → journey with setbacks → result or cliffhanger.

| Beat | Fill-in lines |
|---|---|
| Hook | "I'm going to [challenge] in [time]. Here's what happened." · "Can a total beginner [goal] from [start]? We tried." · "[X] days to [challenge]. No shortcuts." |
| Rules | "The rules: [1], [2], [3]." · "Start: [baseline]. Goal: [target]. Deadline: [date]." |
| Journey | "Day [X]: [setback]." · "Then [complication] and I almost [near-fail]." · "By day [X] I was only at [progress]." |
| Result / cliffhanger | "Final result: [start] to [end] in [time]." · "With [X] hours left… follow for part 2." |

**Dubai Bachata angle:** "Never danced → first social in 4 classes." "Dance with 10 strangers at the Thursday social." "Learn one song's choreography in 7 days." Complications must be real.
**Faceless?** Yes for an animated mascot challenge; real-person versions are stronger.

## 7. Win (victory announcement)
**Starting point:** a specific achievement with proof. Celebrate it; don't teach it.
**Beats:** Hook (the win) → proof → emotional beat → optional nod to the journey → optional CTA.

| Beat | Fill-in lines |
|---|---|
| Hook | "It finally happened: [win]." · "We just [achievement] and I'm still in shock." |
| Proof | "Look at this." · or just hold up the proof |
| Emotion | "You have no idea how long we worked for this." · "I almost quit [when]. This is why I didn't." |
| Acknowledge | "From [before] to [after]." · "None of this happens without [the students / showing up every week]." |
| CTA | "Next goal: [milestone]. Follow to watch." |

**Dubai Bachata angle:** a sold-out Foundation group, a graduation, festival artist confirmed, a follower milestone. Proof only from real numbers `[OWNER: ]`.
**Faceless?** Partly. Proof screen + mascot reaction works; Saj & Emi raw reaction is best.

## 8. Day in the Life
**Starting point:** one day's events, with a complication.
**Beats:** Hook (identity + context) → morning → core work + complication → resolution / wind-down.

| Beat | Fill-in lines |
|---|---|
| Hook | "A day in the life of a [role] in Dubai." · "What a Sunday looks like when you teach bachata." |
| Morning | "Every day starts with [habit] because [reason]." · "Most people don't know I [unexpected detail]." |
| Core work | "Today's priority: [task]. The problem: [complication]." · "Halfway through [task], [problem], so I had to [pivot]." |
| Wind-down | "End of day. Done: [summary]. Tomorrow: [next]." · "This is what [lifestyle] really looks like. Not perfect, but [honest feeling]." |

**Dubai Bachata angle:** Saj & Emi's Thursday: day jobs or prep → playlist building → teaching 3 classes → LCT party at Room 44. Unexpected = what happens before class.
**Faceless?** No. Real footage. Shot list for the Thursday shoot.

## 9. Personal Update
**Starting point:** news from your life or mission. Community-building, not authority.
**Beats:** Hook (tease) → context → the update (with tension) → rationale → optional CTA.

| Beat | Fill-in lines |
|---|---|
| Hook | "I need to tell you something." · "Something big just changed and I want to explain why." · "We've kept this quiet for [time]. It's time." |
| Context | "For the past [time] we've been [previous plan]." |
| Update | "As of [date], we're [change]. It wasn't easy because [tension]." |
| Rationale | "The reason is simple: [reason]." · "[Value] matters more to us than [trade-off]." |

**Dubai Bachata angle:** festival moved to 26–29 March 2027 (replaces the old "Postponed" post), new Foundation group, new practice night, festival reveals.
**Faceless?** Works either way; real faces convert better for trust.

## 10. Lesson From Others (Mentor story)
**Starting point:** someone else's story taught you something. You narrate; they're the hero.
**Beats:** Hook (person + lesson) → who they are → their situation → the lesson (direct quote) → how you applied it → CTA.

| Beat | Fill-in lines |
|---|---|
| Hook | "I learned something powerful from [person]." · "The best advice [person] ever gave me." · "[Person] told me one thing that changed how I [area]." |
| Situation | "They were [struggling / at the top of their game / starting over]." · "When I met them, they had just [event]." |
| Lesson | "They told me: '[quote].'" · "The one thing they said I'll never forget: '[quote].'" |
| Application | "I took that and [action]. Within [time], [outcome]." |
| CTA | "Save this for a hard day." · "Who taught you the most? Comment below." |

**Dubai Bachata angle:** `[OWNER: Saj's teacher, a Dominican dancer or musician]` and the lesson. A student who taught the teachers something. Best heritage-safe format: it credits Dominican sources in their own words.
**Faceless?** Yes, with the main mascot narrating. Name and credit the person (with their OK).
