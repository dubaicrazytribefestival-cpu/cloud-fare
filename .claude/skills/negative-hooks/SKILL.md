# Negative Hooks Skill

97 fill-in-the-blank hook templates in ten families. The templates are reworded and not copied from these sources:
- Families 1–5 come from @alexxmarketing's "50 Negative Hooks" carousel (Oct 2026, "Dark Psychology – use at your own risk").
- Families 6–10 come from @katrinalebar's "52 Negative Hooks that will increase your reach" carousel. Five of its hooks were already covered by families 1–5, so they weren't added again.

Families 1–5 are the edgiest. Families 6–10 are softer and better suited to talking to camera: they sound like a real person's opinion, so they fit the "I did this" proof trend best.

Negative hooks work because people react more strongly to threat, loss and being misled than to gain. Use them to stop the scroll, then deliver real value in the body. The body must keep the promise the hook makes.

## How to use
1. Choose the family that fits the post's message. Don't pick the edgiest family by default.
2. Fill every [bracket] with a specific detail from the user: a number, place, timeframe or real outcome. Never fill one with an invented statistic.
3. Write 3–5 options. Mark which one you recommend and why.
4. Keep each hook to about 12 words or fewer for reel on-screen text, and to one line for a caption.
5. Combine with the proof trend: a negative hook works best when the body is a first-person story ("I did this"), not generic tips. See `SOCIAL MEDIA RESEARCH/2026-10-24_therishishine_content-shift.md`.

## 1. Stakes and loss (what they lose by doing nothing)
1. Every day you ignore [problem], you lose [metric].
2. Keep doing [action] and your [asset] is gone by [date].
3. You've got [timeframe] before [bad event] hits your [asset].
4. Each minute spent on [common habit] pushes [goal] further away.
5. Someone is taking your [resource], and you're paying them to do it.
6. This is the point where you stopped running your [business/life].
7. There are 4 stages of [bad outcome]. Most people are already at stage 3.
8. Watch this before [threat] takes apart your [asset].
9. The cold numbers on why [goal] isn't happening for you.
10. [Option A] isn't just weaker than [Option B]. It's hurting your [result].

## 2. Contrarian and anti-guru (the accepted advice is wrong)
1. What you've been told about [topic] is costing you [result].
2. The [topic] advice you grew up with was never true.
3. [Popular virtue] is the reason you're still [stuck/broke].
4. Being "the nice one" in [niche] is quietly killing your [result].
5. Stop helping [group]. It's sinking your [result].
6. Your favourite [guru/tool] is taking more than it gives. Here's the proof.
7. How [trusted brand] turned your loyalty into their profit.
8. The betrayal in [industry] nobody warned you about.
9. What [authority figure] says off-camera would shock you.
10. The [industry] secret that makes money and shouldn't.

## 3. Curiosity gap and hidden secrets (there's something you can't see)
1. You're not failing at [action] from lack of effort. It's [hidden cause].
2. Your [competitors] are using [tactic] on you right now.
3. I probably shouldn't share how [competitor] takes your [asset].
4. [System] was built to keep you right where you are.
5. The mental trap you walk into every time you [common action].
6. Your own [emotion/trait] is working against you.
7. You keep doing [bad habit] because it was designed that way.
8. Your biggest mistake is in your blind spot right now.
9. A breakdown of the mind games [industry] plays on you.
10. The [name] method: how to make your competition irrelevant.

## 4. Power dynamics (who's really in control)
Brand-safety note: these are the riskiest hooks. Use them to expose manipulation that is done to the audience, never to teach the audience to manipulate others.
1. You think you chose this. You were steered.
2. How [emotion] gets used to make you say yes.
3. The 3-step loop that keeps customers hooked on [product/service].
4. How I beat the [system] to get [result].
5. The trigger that skips straight past your logical brain.
6. The conversation trick that makes people second-guess themselves.
7. You're playing checkers. [Competitor] is playing chess.
8. When [event] arrives, only the prepared make it through.
9. Give me 30 seconds to name the thing holding you back.
10. What separates you from [top performer] is one uncomfortable trait.

## 5. Brutal truths and labels (call the viewer out)
1. It's not bad luck. You're just predictable.
2. Why [audience] doesn't take your [work/product] seriously.
3. Everyone sees your [flaw]. They're just too polite to say so.
4. You don't really understand [topic]. Here's the gap.
5. Still believe [common belief]? Then you're the target.
6. Most [audience] can't handle this truth about [topic].
7. Ditch your [common tool]. It's dragging you down.
8. Your excuses for not having [result] are protecting you from trying.
9. For anyone tired of losing at [situation].
10. The uncomfortable reason part of you wants [goal] to fail.

## 6. Blunt call-out (tell them straight)
1. Doing [activity]? You're burning your time.
2. [Assumed problem] isn't the issue. [Real problem] is.
3. Quit listening to people who [disqualifying trait].
4. Sorry to be the one to say it, but [truth].
5. You won't like this, but [truth].
6. With respect, you're getting [activity] completely wrong.
7. Let's be honest: [popular method] doesn't work.
8. This is the exact reason [struggle] keeps happening to you.
9. You could be getting in the way of your own [goal].

## 7. Unpopular opinion (take a side)
1. I'll never get why people still [behaviour].
2. Hot take: I disagree with [popular view].
3. This may offend a few people, but [opinion].
4. Why are we still celebrating [overrated thing]?
5. We should stop telling people to [common advice].
6. You don't have to [expected thing]. Here's why.
7. This will ruffle some feathers: [opinion].
8. I might lose followers over this, but [opinion].
9. Everyone's doing [trend]. That doesn't mean you should.
10. I think we've had [topic] backwards all along.

## 8. The mistake (what's going wrong)
1. This mistake cost me [real loss].
2. Odds are you're making one of these [topic] mistakes.
3. Please stop doing this if [goal] matters to you.
4. If I see one more [mistake], I'm going to scream.
5. The number one beginner mistake with [topic].
6. Everyone skips the most important part of [topic].
7. I really wish people would stop making this mistake.

## 9. Comparison and reality check (what they're not seeing)
1. It worked for them. That doesn't mean it'll work for you.
2. You're only seeing half of the [topic] story.
3. The part of [topic] everyone conveniently skips.
4. Nobody posts this side of [topic].
5. What you see online about [topic] isn't the whole picture.
6. You might be chasing something that won't make you happy.
7. You don't have to keep up with everyone else.
8. Real [topic] looks nothing like it does on Instagram.
9. What people leave out when they talk about [topic].
10. The truth behind the "perfect" [thing] all over your feed.

## 10. Spicy and polarising (start a debate)
1. Possibly the most annoying truth about [topic].
2. I'm done with people pretending [thing].
3. Can we talk about how ridiculous [thing] is?
4. I honestly don't get why people still [behaviour].
5. Some of you will take this personally.
6. This one's going to upset anyone who [behaviour].
7. I already know why this opinion will make people mad.
8. I don't think people are ready for this conversation.
9. Let's start calling out [thing].
10. Not sugarcoating this one.
11. Let's stop pretending [thing] is normal.

## Examples for events (Weekender / festival)
- Stakes: "Every week you wait, early-bird tickets for [event] get pricier."
- Contrarian: "Everything promoters told us about [festival rule] was wrong."
- Curiosity: "The real reason most festivals in [city] lose money."
- Power: "How I beat the [venue/permit] system to keep [event] alive."
- Brutal truth: "Your crowd isn't bored. Your lineup is predictable."
- Unpopular opinion: "Hot take: VIP tables are ruining Dubai festivals."
- Mistake: "This mistake cost us [X] tickets at the Weekender."
- Reality check: "Nobody posts this side of running a festival."
- Spicy: "Let's stop pretending a 2am finish is a good night out."

## Guardrails
- Never make false claims, invent numbers, or name and accuse a real person or brand without evidence.
- Tone down hooks about insecurity or manipulation for a family or community brand.
- Instagram can limit reach for misleading or shaming content. Make sure the body delivers what the hook promises.
