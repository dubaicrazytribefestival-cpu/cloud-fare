Shared context for every marketing skill in this repo. Read it before asking the owner questions.

## What we sell
- **Dubai Bachata** (@dubaibachata): beginner-first bachata classes by Saj & Emi, using the B-YOU Method, at Dance Case Studio, Fortune Executive Tower, Cluster T, JLT, Dubai.
  - Sunday 8 PM Foundation, 9 PM Improvers; Monday 9–11 PM Practice Night; Thursday 7:30 Foundation, 8:30 Improvers, 9:30 Advanced (owner to confirm Thursday vs Wednesday).
  - No partner needed. Trial AED 50 per the website (confirm before quoting). No other prices are confirmed.
  - B-YOU Path: Foundation → Improvers → Advanced → BachaZouk, with a monthly cohort start and graduations.
- **Latin Crazy Tribe** (@latincrazytribe): Latin party nights. Thursday Room 44, Friday SoBe, Saturday TT DXB.
- **BachaZouk Dubai** (@bachazouk.dubai): BachaZouk workshops, socials and pool parties.
- **Latin Crazy Festival Dubai** (@latincrazyfestival.dubai): 5th edition, 1–5 April 2027 (moved from 26–30 March). Bachafusion weekender, pool, boat and pre-parties, Jack & Jill championship.

## Who we sell to
- Primary: Dubai professionals, mostly 25–45, often expats, who want a social hobby after work and have never danced or stopped years ago. Many come alone.
- Secondary: social dancers who want to level up (Improvers, BachaZouk, festival passes).
- Their fears: looking awkward, not having a partner, being rejected for a dance, being too old or unfit.
- What they want: confidence, new friends, a reason to go out, visible progress.

## Positioning
- "Beginner-first bachata in JLT": no assessment, just come.
- The full path no competitor offers: first class → weekly LCT parties → the LCT Festival stage.
- People-led: Saj & Emi on camera, and students' progress stories.

## Voice
Warm, direct and encouraging, like a teacher who remembers being a beginner. Short sentences. Never shaming. No AI tells, no hype words. Soft negative hooks are allowed (see the negative-hooks skill).
