## Who we sell to
- Primary: Dubai professionals, mostly 25–45, often expats, who want a social hobby after work and have never danced or stopped years ago. Many come alone.
- Secondary: social dancers who want to level up (Improvers Social, BachaZouk, festival passes).
- Their fears: looking awkward, not having a partner, being rejected for a dance, being too old or unfit.
- What they want: confidence, new friends, a reason to go out, visible progress.

## Positioning
- "Beginner-first bachata in JLT": no assessment, just come.
- The full path no competitor offers: first class → weekly LCT parties → the festival stage.
- People-led: Saj & Emi on camera, and students' progress stories.

## Voice and limits
Warm, direct and encouraging, like a teacher who remembers being a beginner. Short sentences. Never shaming. No AI tells, no hype words. Soft negative hooks are allowed (negative-hooks skill).
**Never:** sensual framing; naming or referencing competitors in public content; publishing package or private prices.
