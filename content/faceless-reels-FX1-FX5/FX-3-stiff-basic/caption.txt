Why your bachata basic looks stiff (and the 2-minute fix). Comment BASIC for the drill.

Most stiff basics come from locked knees. When the knees are straight, the only way to get the hip moving is to push it sideways, and that's what looks forced.

The fix is to shift your weight instead:
1. Soft knees, never locked.
2. Put your full weight on each step.
3. Count it slowly: 1-2-3-tap, then 5-6-7-tap the other way.
Don't move the hip on purpose. Let it follow the weight.

Same basic. No robot. 🤖➡️💃
