If that's you, it isn't talent. With a partner, the lead gives you cues and you react. Alone, nobody gives you cues, and 20 memorised combos means 20 choices, so your brain buffers.

Try this at your next social:
1. Basic first, always.
2. Add ONE thing: shoulders, arms, or a pause.
3. Pick one instrument and move to that, instead of thinking about your hips.

And the circle? Research on the "spotlight effect" found people notice us about half as much as we think. They're busy counting their own steps.

If you've ever waited for everyone else to start dancing first, you're in very good company.
