## Step 1: Voice-over
- **Voice:** warm, friendly, a little cheeky, like a dance teacher talking to a nervous beginner. Brisk pace. Neutral international English (British or neutral accent preferred).
- **Same voice for all 5 reels** so the series feels like one brand.
- **Lines:** say every voice-over line in `AUDIO-CUES.md` **word for word**. Don't add or change words.
- **Timing:** each line must start and end within the seconds given. The words are already on screen as captions, so the voice must match them. **The first word of every reel lands at 0.30 s.** This is critical for the hook.
- If a line is too long for its window, speed up the delivery slightly. Never move the line.

## Step 2: Sound effects
- Add every sound effect listed in `AUDIO-CUES.md` at its exact timestamp (record scratch, glass crack, error ding, bell, clank, pops, cheer and so on).
- Keep them short, punchy and playful, and never louder than the voice.

## Step 3: Music
- **Royalty-free or properly licensed instrumental bachata only.** No copyrighted songs, because Instagram will mute or remove the reel.
- Use the **tempo (BPM) given in each cue sheet** so the dancers on screen step on the beat.
- Music sits **under the voice, about -18 dB** while someone is speaking, and comes up a little between lines.
- **Drop-outs:** where the cue sheet says the music stops, cut it to **complete silence** at that exact second, then bring it back where stated.
  - **FX-4 is the most important.** The music must cut to silence at **exactly 2.200 s** and return at **3.000 s**, because that's the "break" the reel is teaching.
- End each reel on a clean musical button at the end card. Where the cue sheet says the reel loops, make the end flow back into the start.

## Step 4: Mix and export
- Voice must be clear on a phone speaker. Normalise to about **-14 LUFS**, with no clipping.
- Export **two versions per reel**:
  1. `FX-#_final.mp4`: voice + music + sound effects
  2. `FX-#_voice-only.mp4`: voice + sound effects, no music (so the owner can add a trending Instagram sound instead)
- Format: 1080×1920 MP4, H.264 video (keep the original picture), AAC audio 48 kHz stereo, under 50 MB each.

## Step 5: Check before handing back
For each reel, confirm:
- [ ] The first spoken word is at 0.30 s
- [ ] Every line matches the on-screen captions in words and timing
- [ ] Music drop-outs land exactly (FX-4 at 2.200 s)
- [ ] The voice is always clearer than the music
- [ ] The picture is unchanged (same length, same frames)
- [ ] The music is licensed or royalty-free (write down its source)
