=== CAPTION (corrected) ===
Everyone's dancing bachata. Almost nobody knows what branched out of it 🌳

Bachata is Dominican 🇩🇴. It's the roots and the trunk. Around the world, other dances grew out of its music:

Moderna: salsa-style turns and patterns
Sensual: waves and body rolls, born in Spain
Fusion: hip hop, tango and new-school flavour
BachaZouk: head movement from zouk

Every one of them traces back to the Dominican Republic. That's why we start every beginner at the roots: body movement, musicality, culture and your first step.

Time + knowledge = mastery.
